Kept: RadioGroup semantics, the custom colour input, the `<output>`. The spec flags the default change as a visible contract change (anatomy and appearance) with its impact, and records conflicts with existing rules (44.8 px coarse target from #431/#452, no handwritten gradients for the multicolour circle, ColorSwatchGroup colour exception) as owner decisions with options.
