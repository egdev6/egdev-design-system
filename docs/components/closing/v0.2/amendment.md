## 1. Purpose and scope

Closing gains the optional `titleVariant?: 'hero' | 'show'` (default `'hero'`) defined in block 3 of the shared spec, and passes it to its Heading as `variant`. Closing is the component of the reported case in #560: in its roughly 516px copy column, "¿CONSTRUIMOS ALGO?" breaks mid-word at the `hero` size. Nothing else about Closing changes: props, the required CTA `children`, decorative `media`, markers, grid, spacing, lede, host register and the 860px boundary stay as approved in v0.1. **Token proposal: none.** The shared TV-09 measurement found that `show` fits that column up to about 1,420px of viewport and breaks above it; the owner chose to ship the prop as specified and let the website shorten its copy at wider viewports (shared OQ-2).
