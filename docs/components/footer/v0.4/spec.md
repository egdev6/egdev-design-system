## 1. Purpose and scope

- **Need / non-goals:** egdev.es wants its footer on the page grid and on the Grafito reading register, and can get neither today without breaking a Footer contract.
  - **#561, width.** At 1440px the footer content starts at x=0 while sections start at the content edge. `footerVariants` sets `p-0 pb-8` (`packages/ui/src/organisms/footer/types.ts:112`). `className` is for outer placement, margin and sizing only (v0.2 §6), so the page gutter, which is padding, cannot be supplied, and a `max-w` plus `mx-auto` on the root would also shrink the root hairlines and any wash to the content width. WebTemplate renders the footer slot "without another wrapper" (`packages/ui/src/templates/web-template/types.ts:20`).
  - **#565, register.** "Pulso en Grafito" lists the footer among the reading sections on the `grafito` register (`docs/DECISIONES.md:92`), with a fade only at the group edge (`docs/MATERIAL.md:78`). Footer paints no background and leaves it to the host (v0.2 §1 and §6). Wrapping it in SectionShell forces `min-h-[var(--section-min-height)]` and the nav offset (`packages/ui/src/templates/section-shell/types.ts:37`) and nests `<footer>` in a `<section>`, which drops `contentinfo` (`footer/types.ts:33`).

  **Exact delta from effective v0.3** (two optional props and one internal link variant; nothing is removed or renamed):
  1. `wrap?: boolean`, default `false`. When `true`, one internal wrapper `div.eg-footer__wrap` (centered, `--content-max`, `--gutter`) encloses the motto stage, the grid and the legal row. The root keeps its full-width top and bottom hairlines and its bottom padding.
  2. `register?: 'none' | 'grafito'`, default `'none'`. `grafito` paints the SectionShell Grafito wash on the footer root. The footer stays a native `footer` (`contentinfo`) at its natural height.
  3. `FooterNavigation` renders its links with Link `variant='quiet'` (neutral text plus underline), unconditionally. This requires Link v0.5 (#562).
  4. Unchanged: every other v0.1 to v0.3 promise: parts and their order, validation and omission rules, `aria-current`, the inclusive 860px breakpoint, the hairlines, the 44.8px link target recipe, the markers other than `eg-footer__wrap`, the `className` surfaces, native props and the absence of refs and `asChild`.

  **Non-goals:** a fade input, underlay part or mask token (host-owned, OQ-2); a minimum height, nav offset or any full-viewport behavior; a `section`, `role` or landmark change; a `contentWidth`-style mode that keeps gutters on a full-width row; a `className` surface change (no `@classNameAllows`); a shared page-wrap abstraction extracted from SectionShell, TopBar and Footer; a Footer prop for the link look; any change to SectionShell, TopBar, WebTemplate, HomeTemplate or Link (Link v0.5 has its own spec); new tokens, dependencies, Radix packages, stylesheets or internal modules; detection of neighboring sections, themes or the ambient layer; migration of any consumer or of the legacy bundle.

  **Changed promises and impact** (the approval gate must see these):

| Promise | Effective text | v0.4 | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Background | "The host owns placement, background and fade" (v0.2 §1); "Footer paints no background, gradient or shadow" (v0.2 §6) | Default unchanged. With `register='grafito'` the root paints the wash. The fade stays host-owned | None unless a consumer passes `register`. A host that already paints a wash behind the footer keeps `none`, or the wash doubles |
| Structure | The motto stage, grid and legal row are direct children of the root; absent regions leave no wrapper (v0.2 §2) | Default unchanged. With `wrap`, exactly one wrapper exists | None unless `wrap` is set |
| Markers | `eg-footer__wrap` and the other legacy part markers stay absent (v0.1 plan line 56; `Footer.test.tsx:98-100`) | `eg-footer__wrap` is emitted only when `wrap` is true; the other legacy markers stay absent | One public marker is added, and the v0.1 legacy inventory is extended for it (OQ-3) |
| Footer link look | `plain`: link-coloured text plus underline (Link v0.4) | `quiet`: neutral text plus underline | A visible change (ink and underline colours) for every Footer consumer once Link v0.5 ships, with no opt-out prop. Footer itself changes neither the type step nor the target recipe |

- **Cataloging decision / alternatives:** Approved (L3): "Footer v0.4 with `wrap` and `register`". Kind: **variant** of the existing organism (two optional props), plus an internal adoption of Link `quiet`. Rejected, with evidence:
  - *Reuse unchanged or a `className` workaround:* the gutter is padding and `className` is outer placement only (v0.2 §6); #561 and #565 both record no mitigation.
  - *Reuse SectionShell as the wrapper:* it forces `min-h-[var(--section-min-height)]` plus the nav offset (`section-shell/types.ts:37`) and renders a `<section>`, so the footer would sit in sectioning content and lose `contentinfo` (`footer/types.ts:33`).
  - *A wrapper in WebTemplate:* WebTemplate promises that the footer slot renders "without another wrapper" (`web-template/types.ts:20`; WebTemplate v0.2 spec, anatomy table, "footer: No extra landmark or wrapper"), it would force gutters on footers that bring their own, and it cannot paint a register for one slot.
  - *Compose in the app:* every consumer would repeat the Grafito recipe by hand, and AGENTS.md treats any Material composition outside Surface or an approved recipe as unapproved.
  - *TopBar's `contentWidth` (`top-bar-content-width`):* two row-width modes that both keep gutters. Footer has no gutters in either mode today, and one wrapper on or off is what #561 asks for.
  - *A new component or a shared page-wrap primitive:* it would add an abstraction across SectionShell, TopBar and Footer for four utilities; out of scope.
  - *`register` as `tone` (`surface-material-tone`):* the wash is SectionShell's Grafito register recipe verbatim, and one concept keeps one name (owner decision, L3).
- **Evidence / level and package:** Organism in `@egdev/ui`, unchanged (`packages/ui/src/organisms/footer/`). Availability comes from the React source and the barrel: `packages/ui/src/index.ts:139-157` exports `Footer`, its parts, `footerHeadingVariants` and the types. Verified current shape: `types.ts:34-41` declares `FooterProps` as the native root props plus `children` (no `wrap`, no `register`); `types.ts:110-113` declares `footerVariants` with no variants; `useFooter.ts:71-92` builds the root props and partitions the parts; `Footer.tsx:21-36` renders `footer > stage, grid, legal`; `useFooter.ts:108-115` and `Footer.tsx:64` build and render each link through `Link` with `arrow={false}`. Precedent read: SectionShell `types.ts:5,14-33,36-56`, `useSectionShell.ts:5-21` and `SectionShell.tsx:8` (the `register` and `wrap` props, the Grafito recipe at line 45, the wrapper recipe at lines 54-56, the wrapper ternary); the legacy bundle `packages/ui/src/styles/legacy/bundle.css:677-697`; tokens `packages/tokens/src/theme.css:498-501` and `:531-533`. Inspection establishes exports and markup, not visual conformity.

  Re-check of the approved cataloging evidence: no contradiction, four precision notes.
  1. **Convention wording.** The owner's widening answer names `section-register`. `section-content-wrap` carries the same section-scoped wording (`skills/_shared/prop-naming.json:8385`), and Footer is not a section: it must stay outside sectioning content (`footer/types.ts:33`). | Part | Responsibility | Native element / Radix primitive | Allowed content |

- **Structure:** with `wrap` false (default) the output equals v0.3: `footer.eg-footer > stage, grid, legal`. With `wrap` true: `footer.eg-footer > div.eg-footer__wrap > stage, grid, legal`. Nothing else is rendered inside the root. Absent regions leave no wrapper, and when no part renders there is no wrapper either (an empty root remains, as today).
- **Radix / composition:** None, as in v0.2. `Link` is reused; its `quiet` value arrives with Link v0.5 and its recipe is not restated here. No `asChild`. No Surface (block 6). No import from SectionShell: an organism does not depend on a template, so the wrapper recipe is declared in Footer's own `types.ts`.
- **Public markers:** `eg-footer`, `eg-footer__stage`, `eg-footer__grid`, `eg-footer__legal` and `footer-brand` stay. **Added: `eg-footer__wrap`**, present only when `wrap` is true (OQ-3). No `eg-footer--*` modifier is emitted for the register (the legacy bundle defines none for the footer, `bundle.css:677-697`), and no `data-register` or other attribute mirrors it.

/** Material register of the footer root. */
export type FooterRegister = 'none' | 'grafito';

export type FooterProps = RootNative & {
  /**
   * Supply a centered content wrapper with the page gutters and content max-width around the motto, grid and
   * legal parts; the root hairlines and bottom padding stay full width. Leave off when the host already

  wrap?: boolean;
  /**
   * Material register of the footer root. none paints no background and the host owns it; grafito paints

   * @default none
   * @control select

  register?: FooterRegister;
  /* children: unchanged */

## 4. Behavior

| Initial state / action | Expected result | Emitted event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neither prop, or `wrap={false}` with `register='none'` | Output identical to v0.3 apart from the link look (Link `quiet`, below): the stage, grid and legal row are direct children of the footer, no `eg-footer__wrap`, no `bg-*` utility on the root. Neither prop reaches the DOM | None |
| `wrap` is true and at least one part renders | One `div.eg-footer__wrap` is the root's only child and holds the stage, grid and legal row in the v0.3 order | None |
| `wrap` is true and no part renders | No wrapper | None |
| `wrap` is not the boolean `true` (plain JS or a cast: `'true'`, `1`) | Behaves as `false`: no wrapper, no error, no warning (proposed; it mirrors Link v0.4, where an out-of-union value falls back to the default) | None |
| `register` is `'grafito'` | The root paints the Grafito wash: `surface-0` at 50% over the host, solid `surface-0` under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`. It stays a native `footer` in normal flow at its natural height | None |
| `register` is absent, `'none'` or outside the union (a cast such as `'pro'`) | No wash: the host owns the background, as in v0.3. No error, no warning | None |
| Both props are set | They are independent: the wash spans the root and the wrapper only constrains content | None |
| The host re-renders with `wrap` toggled | The tree shape changes, so the parts remount. Footer holds no state; a consumer part with local state loses it | None |
| The host wants a group-edge fade | Host-owned and not an API: it paints a masked underlay and suppresses the root wash locally (OQ-2) | None |
| A Footer navigation link is activated | Native navigation, as in v0.3; the anchor carries the `quiet` recipe | Native event only |
| Viewport at or below 860px | The inclusive two-track split is unchanged and still viewport-based. With `wrap`, the content is narrower by two gutters at every width | None |
| Parts, order, omission and validation rules, current page, `className` merge | Unchanged (v0.1 to v0.3) | None |

- **States / representation:** None. `wrap` and `register` select static utilities; there is no `data-state`, no mirrored class and no data attribute.
- **Limits / consumer responsibilities:** The host chooses `wrap` and `register`, keeps the footer outside `main` and sectioning content, and owns the ambient layer behind the wash, any fade, and the contrast of consumer-supplied brand, social and legal content over the wash. A host must not paint its own wash behind a `grafito` footer. Footer does not inspect neighboring sections.

## 5. Accessibility

- **Accessible name / roles / relationships:** Unchanged. A native `footer` is `contentinfo` outside `main` and sectioning content (v0.2 §5), and neither prop changes the element or role: that is the point of #565, since a SectionShell wrapper would drop the landmark. The wrapper is a generic `div` with no role, name, id or `aria-*`. The wash is paint only. Navigation names, heading ids (`useId()`), `aria-current` and link names are unchanged.
- **Keyboard / focus:** Unchanged and native: Tab, Shift+Tab and Enter. The wrapper adds no tab stop and declares no `overflow`, so a link's focus ring (`--shadow-focus`, owned by Link) is not clipped at the wrapper edge, which keeps at least `--gutter` (12.8px at the minimum) of room.
- **Announcements / other conditions:** Footer announces nothing and adds no live region. Non-colour cue: Footer-owned links are identified by the underline of Link `quiet`, whose recipe belongs to Link v0.5. Footer takes whatever Link v0.5 approves, and the cue holds only if the resting underline stays (OQ-5). Contrast: the text roles keep their tokens. On the reduced-transparency solid `surface-0` fallback the documented ratios apply (`fg-muted` 7.17:1, `fg-secondary` 13.1:1 on canvas, `theme.css:72-76`). Over the default translucent wash the result depends on the host's ambient layer, is **not guaranteed by Footer** (the same boundary as SectionShell SS-08), and is measured on the real site, not here. Targets: Footer-owned links keep the 44.8 × 44.8px recipe; consumer-supplied links stay consumer-owned. Reduced transparency gets the solid fallback; no motion is added. Forced colors and screen-reader output are not evaluated.

| Variant axis | Allowed values | Default or rule | Combinations / restrictions |

- **Tokens / Material:**
  - `none`: no background paint, as in v0.3; the hairlines are unchanged.
  - `grafito` (recipe proposed here, for Footer only): the `register` axis of `footerVariants` in `types.ts` (CVA, composed per part with `cn()`) adds `bg-surface-0/50 bg-none [@media(prefers-reduced-transparency:reduce)]:bg-surface-0`. These are the background utilities of `sectionShellVariants.register.grafito` (`section-shell/types.ts:45`), verbatim. The root already carries `text-fg-default` (`types.ts:112`), so none is added. Token: `--color-surface-0`. No shadow, gradient, handwritten colour mix or arbitrary value: the 50% mix is Tailwind's opacity modifier on a token, as in SectionShell, and the Footer's own hairlines remain its boundary (v0.2 §6). Material semantics: a flat canvas wash (`docs/MATERIAL.md:77`), not an elevated surface. Surface cannot express it (solid, bordered `surface-0`), hence a recipe approved here. Approving this spec approves it for Footer only: not for another component and not as a general wash recipe.
  - Natural height: no `min-h`, flex centering, `isolate` or nav compensation. Those belong to SectionShell and stay out of scope.
  - Wrapper: `footerWrapVariants` is `eg-footer__wrap box-border w-full max-w-[var(--content-max)] mx-auto px-[var(--gutter)]`, the utilities of `sectionShellWrapVariants` (`section-shell/types.ts:54-56`). It reads `--content-max` (1024px) and `--gutter` (`clamp(12.8px, 3.2vw, 32px)`) (`theme.css:498-501`). At 1440px that is a 1024px box starting at x=208px with 32px gutters, so the content starts at x=240px and is 960px wide (derived from the tokens, not measured). The legacy `.eg-footer__wrap` rule has the same four declarations (`bundle.css:678`); the bundle is imported into `@layer components`, which precedes `utilities` (`packages/ui/src/styles/index.css:10-12`), so utilities win and no neutralization is needed. FT-22 must prove that with the legacy bundle loaded.
  - Hairlines in wrap mode: the root's top and bottom hairlines stay full width. The grid-top and legal-top hairlines now sit inside the wrapper and span the content box (OQ-4).
  - Footer links: ink, underline and type step come from Link `quiet`; the Link v0.5 spec, approved by the same owner answer (L5), gives neutral `text-fg-default` ink, a neon underline and the unchanged `text-label-default` step (its OQ-1 to OQ-3, resolved with their recommended answers). A `HostFade` story would document it; it is not API.
  - No token, dependency or Radix package is proposed. Nothing in `packages/tokens/src/theme.css` changes.
- **Dynamic geometry:** Not applicable: no inline appearance or interpolated class is involved.
- **Class composition / consumer layout:** Unchanged. The root and every part keep `cn(recipe, className)` for outer placement, margin and sizing only. The wrapper is internal and has no `className` input, and no `@classNameAllows` tag is added. `wrap` and `register` are the appearance API; a consumer `bg-*` class is not a supported way to repaint the root. FT-26 tests the merge.
