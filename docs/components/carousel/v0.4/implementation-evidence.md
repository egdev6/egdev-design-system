## 1. Settled geometry

Shipped: **`px-6 pt-5 pb-7`** and `rounded-xl`, with `--shadow-glow-base`. The first pass shipped `px-6 pt-7 pb-12` with `--shadow-glow-active`; the spec's approved starting point is `px-6 pt-6 pb-10`. **`pt-7 pb-12` can shrink** to `pt-5 pb-7` (22.4 px less track height: 6.4 at the top, 16 at the bottom), and the starting point can shrink by 12.8 px.

- **Method (the first pass's, unchanged).** A static `storybook-build` of the working tree, served from a scratch Node server and read with Playwright-core. Dark Grafito canvas `rgb(20, 21, 24)`, device scale 1, fonts loaded, overlay scrollbars. For each candidate the track padding was overridden in the page (evidence only, no source change), the screenshot was decoded, and the clipped tail was read at the first fully inside pixel row or column of each track edge against the flat canvas just outside, averaged over a 21 px band, as the largest 8-bit channel departure and as CIEDE2000 (ΔE00). My CIEDE2000 was checked against five published reference pairs (Sharma, Wu and Dalal) and matches to four decimals. Three scenarios (Surface slides in a 1248 px root, the same at a 320 px root, TipCard slides in the 307 px poster host) × two content modes (`typical`: glow plus the content's own shadows; `glow-only`: content shadows removed). The vertical sweep was a full cross product, which also showed that the top step depends only on `pt` and the bottom step only on `pb`. The criterion is the one the owner's delegation used: every edge below ΔE00 about 1.0. The spec leaves numeric tolerances to the plan ("agreed in the plan") and the plan states none, so this is the figure the task set; at the 8-bit floor it means at most one level of 255 at the clip edge.
- **Vertical sweep (glow-base; worst over the 3 scenarios, 2 modes and every other padding; ΔE00 and levels of 255):**

  | `pt` | top | `pb` | bottom |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `pt-3` 9.6 px | 1.95 (2) | `pb-5` 16 px | 3.48 (4) |
  | `pt-4` 12.8 px | 1.30 (1) | `pb-6` 19.2 px | 1.95 (2) |
  | **`pt-5` 16 px** | **0.68 (1)** | **`pb-7` 22.4 px** | **0.68 (1)** |
  | `pt-6` 19.2 px | 0 (0) | `pb-8` 25.6 px | 0 (0) |
  | `pt-7` 22.4 px | 0 (0) | `pb-10` 32 px, `pb-12` 38.4 px | 0 (0) |

  The smallest token steps under the criterion are `pt-5` and `pb-7`. `pt-6 pb-8` is the zero-step alternative (+3.2 px at each edge). Track height with Surface slides (slide 74.72 px): `pt-5 pb-7` 113.1 px, `pt-6 pb-8` 119.5 px, the starting point `pt-6 pb-10` 125.9 px, the first pass `pt-7 pb-12` 135.5 px; the default track is 85.5 px.
- **Sides (glow-base; worst over the 3 scenarios; ΔE00 and levels):**

  | `px` | `glow-only` | `typical` | C1 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `px-4` 12.8 px | 1.95 (2) | 1.93 (3) | holds |
  | `px-5` 16 px | 1.30 (1) | 1.33 (2) | holds |
  | **`px-6` 19.2 px** | **0.68 (1)** | **1.23 (4)** | holds |
  | `px-7` 22.4 px | 0.29 (1) | 1.82 (7) | **violates** (equals the gap: neighbours touch the scrollport) |

  `px-6` is the largest token that satisfies C1 and the smallest that keeps the glow's own side tail under 1.0, so it stays. The `typical` column is higher than `glow-only` at the sides because of the content, not the glow (F2).
- **C1.** Padding 19.2 px against `column-gap` 22.4 px: a 3.2 px margin, above the hook's 0.5 px alignment tolerance. At rest the neighbours sit 3.19 and 3.22 px outside the scrollport at the 1248 px and 320 px roots and 3.00 and 3.59 px outside in the 307 px host, after Next, ArrowRight and Home; no neighbour content pixel is in the gutters.
- **C2.** The vertical tail left at the shipped values is one 8-bit level (ΔE00 0.68) in all three scenarios and both modes. `pt-5` is exactly the estimated glow reach above (16 px) and `pb-7` is 1.6 px under the estimated reach below (24 px): the far tail under it is below one level, which is why the criterion, not the estimate, decides.
- **C3.** With classic scrollbars the track has the same 15 px horizontal bar as `Default`, no vertical bar and no vertical overflow (`scrollHeight` equals `clientHeight`) at 1280 and 352 px viewports, for Surface and TipCard slides.
- **First pass, kept as history (glow-active):** at the starting point the clipped tail was top 1.95, bottom 2.62 and sides 3.6 to 5.5 (3 to 7 of 255 levels). The vertical edges were settled to 0.68 with `pt-7 pb-12`; the sides could not be reduced within C1. That is finding F1 and the reason for OQ-2 B.

## 5. Findings

1. **F1 — AC-05 (c), the glow clipped at the track's side edges (resolved by OQ-2 B).** With `--shadow-glow-active` the clipped tail was ΔE00 3.6 to 5.5 at the sides and C1 forbade more side padding (first pass, section 1). The owner chose B (ODD L10; [`approval.md`](approval.md) Amendment 1). With `--shadow-glow-base` the glow's own tail is ΔE00 0.68, one level of 255, at every edge, and the vertical padding shrank from `pt-7 pb-12` to `pt-5 pb-7`. **A correction to the first pass:** the first-pass report quoted 0.56 to 1.02 at the sides for glow-base, taken from a page-level override of the current slide's `box-shadow`; on the real build the typical rendering measures 0.98 to 1.23 (F2). The override figure was an estimate and I did not establish why it was lower; the real-build numbers are what this file uses. The direction of the decision holds (3.6 to 5.5 before, 0.98 to 1.23 now, and 0.68 for the glow alone).
2. **F2 — typical content is slightly above 1.0 at the sides, and the glow is not the cause.** Attribution on the real build, pixel by pixel at the left clip edge (Surface slides, 1248 px root): with no glow and no content shadows the edge is the flat canvas `(20, 21, 24)` (no step); the glow alone gives `(20, 20, 23)` (one level, ΔE00 0.68); the content's shadows alone (glow forced off) give `(18, 19, 21)` (3 levels, ΔE00 1.02); both together give `(18, 18, 20)` (4 levels, ΔE00 1.23). The content part is the `--shadow-elev-1` of Surface and TipCard, mostly the *neighbouring* slide's, which pokes into the gutter because C1 leaves the neighbour's box only 3.2 px outside the scrollport. It is consumer content paint, outside the recipe, and the default track clips content shadows too (variant off: top 0.29, bottom 1.41 with 5 levels, sides 0.56, from `p-2`). It cannot be reduced here: `px-6` is the largest padding C1 allows. Reported so the owner sees the real number, not as an open decision.
3. **F3 — padding choice and the zero-step alternative.** `pt-5 pb-7` is the smallest pair under the criterion, one level (ΔE00 0.68) at the top and bottom. `pt-6 pb-8` (119.5 px track) measures 0 at both edges; the spec's starting point `pt-6 pb-10` measures 0 too. Say if you prefer a zero step over the smaller track; it is a one-line change in `types.ts` plus the pins and the registry digest.
4. **F4 — the plan's `*4` host utility claim.** Plan section 6 says the `w-full max-w-[calc(var(--space-24)*4)]` host exists only in the preview build. `.max-w-[calc(var(--space-24)*4)]` already compiles in the baseline preview stylesheet (other stories use it), so its pin in `storybook-css.test.ts` is a preserve-style assertion with no RED. The story is correct either way.
5. **F5 — forced colours paint the reserved border.** As the spec predicted, under forced colours the transparent border becomes a visible 1 px CanvasText border on every slide, so the frame around each slide shows even for non-current slides; the current slide gets the extra Highlight outline. Consistent across slides, as AC-08 requires. Emulation only.
6. **F6 — focus and glow.** At rest the plain `aria-[current=true]:shadow-*` utility ties with the plain focus rule in specificity, which is why the compound focus rule exists; the browser run shows the compound rule wins and lists the ring layers first.
7. **F7 — edit surfaces and drift.** `packages/ui/src/lib/carousel-docgen.test.ts` was not in the first edit-surface list but the plan names it (section 2); the parent approved adding it. Plan line numbers had drifted because #558 and #559 changed the shared files, so every edit was located by content.
