## 4. Findings

- **F1: in forced colours, keyboard focus on the current page cannot be told from its resting state (plan Risk 3, confirmed).** The spec gives the focused link and the current page the same outline, and Chrome computes `outline: solid 2px` for both the unfocused and the focused current page. Focus on every other link is visible (the outline appears) and the current page is distinguishable from the others, so PG-14 holds; a distinct focus treatment on the current page would be a spec amendment and nothing was changed.
- **F2: in forced colours the decorative ellipses and the placeholders are boxed like links.** All controls share the item recipe with its transparent border, which forced colours paint (`rgb(0, 0, 0)` on the ellipses and placeholders, `rgb(0, 0, 159)` on links, measured on `Window`); that shared border is also what keeps a placeholder as wide as the link it stands for (the row is 387.28px for every `value`). The two colours differ, but a reader may still take an ellipsis for a control. An additive `forced-colors:` border reset on those two recipes would fix it, but spec block 5 and plan choice (d) did not ask for it, so it is the owner's call.
- **F3: Chromium names Previous and Next in uppercase** (`PREVIOUS`, `NEXT`) because the recipe uppercases them with CSS, while the DOM text is exactly as supplied (unit-tested); how a screen reader speaks them is Not evaluated. The numbered links are named `Page n` through `aria-label`.
- **F4: the spec's hand-computed rest-text figure differs from the measurement.** Spec block 5 estimates `fg-secondary` at 13.1:1 on the canvas; the browser gives 11.37:1 (and 10.60:1 on surface-1). Both clear 4.5:1, so the promise holds. The other estimates held: the border 4.61 and 4.30 against the hosts exactly, about 3.5 against the fill (3.73 to 4.00 measured), white text about 14 on the fill (14.75 to 15.84).
- **F5: the DevTools accessibility tree does not list `aria-current`.** The attribute is verified in the DOM only; how Chromium maps it to platform APIs and how a screen reader speaks it are Not evaluated.
- **F6: a Storybook 10.6 deprecation warning appears after a click on `ClientRouting`.** `Accessing the Story Store is deprecated and will be removed in 9.0` is logged once after a click when `action()` receives the React event. The existing `Link` `States` and `IconButton` `States` stories log the same line after a click, Breadcrumb's `ClientRouting` (which passes `action()` a plain object) and Pagination's `Default` log nothing, and no story logs anything on load. It comes from the actions helper, not from the component.
- **F7: hover and the pointer media features.** The first probe run had `hover: none`, so its hover values were discarded and re-measured in the second Chrome described in section 3.
- **F8: two mutations were made in the working tree, not on scratch copies.** Plan section 4 asks for the failing-focus proof on a copy outside the worktree. I removed one utility from `types.ts` for one E2 run, and removed the barrel line for the packed RED run, then restored both byte-for-byte with equal SHA-256 before and after (`b947a96e…`, `ff262947…`); `props:check` and the registry revisions stayed valid.
- **F9: sizes and slicing.** `Pagination.test.tsx` is 1,204 lines for 136 tests (the plan said about 600), and `storybook-css.test.ts` needed a selector-list-aware `emitted()` helper because `rule()` cannot read grouped selector lists. The plan's seven slices mix files (the suites and the integration files share edits), so the proposed grouping is the five commits of the earlier lanes (see the report).
- **F10: changelogs and the README digest.** The ui `## 0.11.0` and system `## 0.13.0` bullets cite `(#571)`; the system bullet says "approved, with their receipt" because D2 was granted. No `effects` or `mcp` entry exists: those changelogs are outside this lane's surfaces and carry only version-follow lines. `pnpm mcp:readme` changed one line of `packages/mcp/README.md` (the response digest), which plan section 2 did not expect.
- **F11: registry wording.** The two entries carry the spec's `meaning` verbatim and its `whenToUse` and `avoid` clauses in sentence case with a final period (plan section 5.1, confirmed by D2); the receipt binds those exact forms. The `navigation-intent` page-number payload is recorded as a usage only, because that entry's text, hash and status do not change.
