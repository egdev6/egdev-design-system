## Proposed API summary (details in the snapshot)

- Root `CommunityCard` (`article` over the raised `surface-1` frame with a Material `surface-2` header band): `title` (required), `level` (2–6, default 3), `eyebrow?` (relationship line through the public `Text variant="eyebrow"`), free body `children`, plus `className` for outer layout.
- Parts: `CommunityCardMark` (decorative consumer slot with an accessible contract: one non-interactive element, named via `role="img"` + `aria-label` or `img` + `alt`, or decorative via `alt=""` — covers the web's `role="img"` wordmark) and `CommunityCardAction` (exactly one consumer `Button`/`Link`, pinned to the bottom edge).
- Reuses `surfaceVariants` (raised `surface-1` frame, `surface-2` band per `docs/MATERIAL.md` bandas), `Text` (eyebrow), `Button`/`Link` and the token type recipes; no new dependency, no Radix primitive of its own, no stylesheet.
- Registry: applied approved entries `heading-title`, `heading-level`, `heading-eyebrow`; the supplied action follows the approved `trailing-actions` concept without declaring an own prop. **No new convention and no exception.**
- `No stylesheet`: every role is expressible with token-backed utilities over the public recipes. A built-in three-part wordmark would need its own approved mark contract (OQ-2), not a local recipe.

## 1. Purpose and scope

- **Need:** one reusable community card in `@egdev/ui` so a new web view can invite readers into a community, server or collaboration as a self-contained unit. The card carries a role line, an optional mark (logo image or consumer-supplied named mark), a display name, free body copy and one action the consumer supplies. No community name, role word, description, mark artwork, URL or call-to-action text is built in: everything arrives through props or slots.
- **Non-goals:** not the grid (`CommunityGrid` is an approved **composition recipe** arranging these cards, not an export); not a social-profile row or icon strip (`SocialLinks`); not a project, article or current-status card; not a built-in three-part typographic wordmark (the web's `STACK & FLOW` box is brand artwork; the card accepts it only as consumer-supplied mark content, OQ-2); no hover or press state on the card itself (the action owns the interaction); no member counts, avatars, networks, invite codes, links, analytics or data fetching; no per-view layout decisions such as column counts or equal heights.
- **Cataloging decision / alternatives:** **new** molecule, per [cataloging-draft.md §4](../../web-catalog/v0.1/cataloging-draft.md) and the `CommunityCard` row in `docs/CATALOGO.md` (action `mover`, source `CommunityCard (molécula)`, composes `Surface, Avatar, Button`). Rejected alternatives: *reuse* (`ListItem card` is a generic content container with media, meta and status snapshots and no display-name plus call-to-action composition; `ModuleCard` is a passive module summary with a status badge and a utility footer; `ProjectCard` and `ArticleFeature` are passive/interactive content cards with different anatomy); *variant* (adding a display name, a role line and a call to action to `ModuleCard` or `ListItem` would change their published responsibilities and unions); *compose* (a `Surface` + heading + supplied `Button` spelled out per view re-implements the same role line, mark, name, copy and pinned action, which is the repetition the approved web-consumer amendment removes); *extract* (nothing inside an existing component hides a community card).

## 2. Anatomy and composition

| Part | Responsibility | Native element / Radix primitive | Allowed content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CommunityCard` root | Article frame, banded Material surface and the display heading | `article` composed over the public `surfaceVariants` raised recipe | The header band, then the body zone (heading, free copy, action part) |
| Header band (internal) | Tonal header above the body, per Material's bandas rule for this card | Internal `div` over `surfaceVariants({ tone: 'surface-2', elevation: 0, padding: 'none', radius: 'none' })` + top radius and bottom hairline | The top row only |
| Top row (internal) | Role line and the mark on the same edge row inside the band | Internal `div` | `Text variant="eyebrow"` and/or `CommunityCardMark`, separated to the edges |
| Role line (`eyebrow` prop) | Context of the relationship with the community | Public `Text variant="eyebrow"` (owns the neon diamond) | One short plain text run; omitted when not supplied |
| `CommunityCardMark` | Community mark on the trailing edge of the top row | Decorative slot (`div`) | Exactly one consumer-supplied, non-interactive mark element: an `img` (with `alt`, or `alt=""` when the mark repeats the visible name) or a named `role="img"` element such as the web's wordmark box; no links, buttons or tab stops |
| Heading (`title` + `level`) | Community name and the card's accessible name | `h2`–`h6` selected by `level` | One plain text run; blank titles warn in development and render nothing |
| Body (`children`) | Free descriptive copy in reading order | Internal `div` | Paragraphs and phrasing content; no headings, no interactive content |
| `CommunityCardAction` | Pins the card's single action to the bottom edge | Internal `div` | Exactly one consumer `Button` (optionally `asChild` with an anchor) or one `Link` |

- **Radix / composition:** no Radix primitive is needed for the card itself: it is passive, holds no keyboard model and no state, so nothing is reimplemented. The single interaction belongs to the consumer's `Button`/`Link`, which already compose Radix `Slot` for `asChild`; an external destination keeps the native `target`/`rel` the consumer sets. The role line reuses the public `Text` eyebrow primitive (with its token diamond) instead of cloning that recipe, and the banded frame reuses the public `surfaceVariants` recipes (`ModuleCard`/`Panel` band precedent). No `asChild` on the card, no ref and no new dependency.
- **Public markers:** `eg-community-card` is the stable public root; parts keep `eg-community-card__band` and `eg-community-card__action`; the top row, the eyebrow and the body wrapper are internal and are not a public override surface. No existing ClassBEM/legacy consumer is migrated: the web keeps `.eg-community-card`/`.dc-card` until an explicitly approved parity migration.
- **Reading order:** DOM order equals visual order — role line, mark, display name, copy, action. The mark sits beside the role line inside the band and never precedes it for assistive technology beyond DOM order, which is the visible order.

## 3. Public API

| Prop / event | Type | Required | Default or rule | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | `string` | yes | — | Visible community name and the card's accessible name. Blank or whitespace-only warns in development and renders nothing (never an unnamed article). |
| `level` | `2 \| 3 \| 4 \| 5 \| 6` | no | `3` | Native heading rank only; the display typography never follows the rank. |
| `eyebrow` | `string` | no | none (omitted) | Relationship line above the name (for example the consumer's own role wording). Whitespace-only renders nothing. |
| `children` | `ReactNode` | no | none (omitted) | Free descriptive copy in reading order; no interactive content and no headings. |
| `className` | `string` | no | — | Outer layout only (grid placement, width, margin), merged last through `cn()`. |
| `CommunityCardMark.children` | `ReactElement` | yes (on the part) | — | Exactly one consumer-supplied mark element. Contract: non-interactive (no anchor, button or tab stop) and either named — `role="img"` with `aria-label`, or `img` with `alt` — or decorative (`img` with `alt=""`/`aria-hidden`) when the mark repeats the visible name. |
| part `className` | `string` | no | — | Local layout only per part; no appearance overrides and no hidden content. |
| `CommunityCardAction.children` | `ReactElement` | yes (on the part) | — | Exactly one action: a `Button` (with `asChild` for an anchor destination) or a `Link`. Two competing actions are outside the contract. |

- **Prop conventions:** [canonical registry](../../../../skills/_shared/prop-naming.json) and [generated view](../../../prop-naming.md), looked up by meaning, name and alias before drafting. Applied approved entries: `title` → `heading-title`; `level` → `heading-level`; `eyebrow` → `heading-eyebrow`. `src`/`alt` are native image attributes of the consumer's own mark element, and part `className`/`children` are inherited props, so neither needs an own convention. The supplied-action part documents the approved `trailing-actions` concept but declares no own prop, because the element itself arrives through `children`. **No new convention is proposed and no existing entry is widened.**
- **Native props / refs:** the root forwards native `article` attributes (`data-*`, `aria-*` outside the protected set) and is merged last through `cn()` for outer layout only. `role`, `tabIndex`, `aria-label`/`aria-labelledby` (the heading names the card), `aria-pressed`/`aria-selected` and `style` are protected, following the `ProjectCard` policy. No ref and no `asChild` on the card: it is passive and its children are not cloned.
- **Controlled / uncontrolled:** Not applicable. The card holds no state, no timers and no controls; the supplied action keeps its own native state.
- **Compatibility:** new export surface (`CommunityCard`, `CommunityCardMark`, `CommunityCardAction` and their props types) added to `packages/ui/src/index.ts` under Molecules. Nothing existing changes; no token, dependency or legacy-class migration is proposed. React 18/19 consumption is a plan gate, not a claim of this draft. The catalog row's `composes` basis (`Surface, Avatar, Button`) is substantively stale on `Avatar` (the mark is a consumer-supplied part, not a fixed identity disc); updating that row is a catalog decision, recorded as OQ-5 with the generator change deferred to a separately approved future plan.

## 4. Behavior

| Initial state / action | Expected result | Emitted event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rendered with `title` only | Header band, heading and nothing else; no empty top row, no empty body block and no action zone | none |
| `eyebrow` supplied | Role line with the token neon diamond on the leading edge of the top row, inside the `surface-2` band | none |
| `CommunityCardMark` supplied beside the eyebrow | Mark on the trailing edge of the top row, vertically aligned with the role line | none |
| Mark without eyebrow, or eyebrow without mark | The top row renders with the single child; nothing else appears in its place | none |
| `title` blank or whitespace-only | Development warning; the card renders nothing at all | none |
| Long `title` or long copy | The name and the copy wrap at word boundaries inside the frame; nothing is truncated, clamped or replaced by a tooltip | none |
| `CommunityCardAction` supplied | Exactly one action pinned to the bottom edge of the card, so cards with unequal copy keep their actions aligned when the host stretches them | The consumer's own action event |
| Consumer activates the action | The `Button`/`Link` performs its own native or router navigation/new tab; the card itself stays non-interactive | Consumer's native event |
| Pointer over the card body | No movement, no tonal change and no pointer cursor: the card is passive and only its action is interactive | none |
| `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` | No change: the card defines no transition, transform or animation of its own | none |

- **States / representation:** no own `data-state` and no mirrored state class. The root exposes `data-level` for debugging only; the only interactive presentation belongs to the supplied action.
- **Limits / consumer responsibilities:** the consumer supplies every string, the mark element and its accessible naming decision (named or decorative), the destination and the action variant (primary or secondary), and decides the grid, column count and equal-height stretching. The card never fetches invite data, renders member counts, localizes copy or reorders content.

## 5. Accessibility

- **Accessible name / roles / relationships:** the root is a native `article` named by its generated heading through `aria-labelledby` with a `useId()` id; `level` keeps the surrounding outline valid. The role line is readable text, not decoration. The mark is consumer-supplied content with a documented contract: a named mark (`role="img"` + `aria-label`, or `img` + `alt`) participates in the card's content — that is exactly how the web's `role="img"` wordmark box behaves — and a mark that repeats the visible name must be decorative (`img` with `alt=""`/`aria-hidden`) so the card is not announced twice. Nothing in the card depends on colour alone.
- **Keyboard / focus:** the card adds no tab stop and no key handler. The only tab stop is the supplied action, which owns its own focus ring and its own hover/press affordances; there is no nested control and therefore no overlapping activation. The mark contract forbids focusable content inside the slot.
- **Announcements / other conditions:** no live region and no synthesized announcement. The action's own announcements (for example a new-tab notice from `Link external`) stay with that component. Touch: the action keeps its own target size (`Button` md is at least 48 px high); the card never shrinks or enlarges it, and the card body is not a target.
- **Consumer obligations:** the heading rank must fit the surrounding outline; the action must be the only interactive element inside the card; copy must not include headings that would compete with the generated `h*`; the mark element must follow the named-or-decorative contract above.

## 6. Appearance

| Variant axis | Allowed values | Default or rule | Combinations / restrictions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `level` | `2`–`6` | `3` | Rank never changes the display typography |

- **Tokens / Material:** the root composes the public `surfaceVariants({ tone: 'surface-1', elevation: 1, padding: 'none', radius: 'xl' })` recipe plus own layout classes (`flex min-w-0 flex-col`). The header band is the documented banded-header geometry (`ModuleCard`/`Panel` precedent): `surfaceVariants({ tone: 'surface-2', elevation: 0, padding: 'none', radius: 'none' })` with `rounded-t-xl` and a bottom hairline (`border-b border-border-hairline`), holding the top row with token padding — exactly the `surface-2` header band `docs/MATERIAL.md` (“En la web (Pulso)”, bandas) prescribes for CommunityCard. The body zone keeps the frame's large padding step (`p-6 md:p-8`, the narrow-host step matching the web's 24 px below 760 px) with `flex flex-col gap-4`. Typography uses existing recipes only: the role line through the public `Text variant="eyebrow"` primitive, the name through `text-title-section text-fg-default` (display family, uppercase, fluid clamp), the copy through `text-body-default text-fg-secondary`, and the action through the supplied `Button`. The mark is consumer content sized by its own contract (the web's image logo is 64 px high); the card constrains it with `max-h-16 max-w-full shrink-0 object-contain` on the slot. The web's glass paint plus `--shadow-card` is replaced by the solid tonal banded frame Material prescribes for the grey register — an allowed difference, not a new recipe; the web's glass card shows no visible band, so the band is a Material-driven addition recorded as such (OQ-1). The boxed wordmark is not built: it arrives as consumer mark content under the OQ-2 contract.
- **Dynamic geometry:** Not applicable. No custom property, no interpolated class string and no inline geometry is introduced; the mark keeps its intrinsic aspect inside the slot.
- **Motion:** none on the card. The supplied action keeps its own transitions, and any future card-level motion must be removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Class composition / consumer layout:** the root merges `className` last through `cn()` for outer placement, width, margin and equal-height stretching; parts merge their own `className` last for local layout. `twMerge` resolves recognised utility conflicts only: there is no universal last-class-wins promise, no appearance-override permission and no per-instance recipe. `surfaceVariants` remains the frame and band appearance API.
- **Files / pending proposals:** the six mandatory files are planned at `packages/ui/src/molecules/community-card/` — `types.ts` (CVA recipes, `CommunityCardProps`, `CommunityCardMarkProps`, `CommunityCardActionProps`, English JSDoc with `@control` on every own declaration), `useCommunityCard.ts` (title/eyebrow normalization, protected props, derived heading id, per-part `cn()`), `CommunityCard.tsx` (JSX only), `CommunityCard.test.tsx`, `CommunityCard.stories.tsx` (`Molecules/CommunityCard`, English) and `index.ts`. **No stylesheet** and no extra internal module. No token or dependency change is proposed; the band uses the existing `surfaceVariants` tones, and a built-in three-part wordmark would need its own approved mark contract (OQ-2), not a local recipe.
